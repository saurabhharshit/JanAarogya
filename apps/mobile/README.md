# JanAarogya (जनआरोग्य) 🏥

An offline-first, multilingual rural healthcare mobile application built with Flutter. JanAarogya bridges critical healthcare accessibility gaps in rural communities through local-language voice assistance, emergency dispatch, maternal care tracking, medicine reminders, and facility discovery.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **🚨 One-Touch Emergency Dispatch:** Instant direct dialing to 108 Emergency Ambulance services and registered family emergency contacts.
* **💊 Exact Medicine Reminders:** Device-level scheduled alarms powered by `flutter_local_notifications` that ring even when offline or when the app is closed.
* **🤰 Maternal & Pregnancy Care Tracker:** Accurate gestational age calculation from LMP (Last Menstrual Period), trimester-based nutrition/diet guidance, recommended exercises, and high-risk warning flags.
* **🏥 Nearby Medical Facilities:** Directory of Community Health Centers (CHCs), Primary Health Centers (PHCs), and Jan Aushadhi pharmacies with one-tap calling and route navigation via Google Maps.
* **👥 Multi-Profile Management:** Offline-first local profiles for entire households, stored securely using local persistence.
* **🔊 Voice & Accessibility Support:** Built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) voice readouts in Hindi for low-literacy and elderly users.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

* **Framework:** Flutter / Dart
* **Local Notifications & Alarms:** `flutter_local_notifications`, `timezone`, `flutter_timezone`
* **Audio & Voice:** `flutter_tts`
* **External Actions & Deep Linking:** `url_launcher`
* **Local Storage:** SQLite / Shared Preferences
